About me

I’m David Dwyer, a mechanical engineering student who designs competition robots, machines carbon fiber, and builds things that move. I was Design Lead for two FIRST Tech Challenge teams, where our robots were made mostly from parts we designed and manufactured ourselves. Most recently, I designed a brake pedal assembly for the Texas A&M Formula SAE EV chassis subteam’s design challenge.

Mechanical Engineering, Texas A&M. Based in College Station and Dallas, Texas.
