Frame and systems

I fabricated the frame and integrated four systems that had to work together:

  • Drivetrain: mechanical drive from the engine to the rear wheels
  • Steering: steering linkage and alignment
  • Electrical: integrated the kart’s electrical system
  • Safety: roll cage, seat mounting, and reliable brakes, all needed before going fast

Most of the work was machining, assembly, alignment, and troubleshooting the structure. I found that getting everything square and aligned mattered as much as horsepower.

Tuning for speed

  • Engine: replaced the carburetor and exhaust for more power
  • Controls: built custom brake and throttle assemblies to match the new power
  • Driver interface: added a butterfly steering wheel for better control at speed
